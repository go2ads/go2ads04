Business website checklist: what your website should include

A practical checklist for a business website: clear messaging, mobile usability, service pages, trust signals, contact options and basic SEO.

A practical guide from go2ads. Information is educational; the right approach depends on your business, market and goals.

A clear first-screen message

Tell visitors what you do, who you help and the next step. Avoid making people guess what the business offers.

Dedicated service information

Give each important service a clear explanation, suitable examples and a relevant contact action. Keep claims factual and current.

Mobile-friendly layouts

Check navigation, text size, buttons, forms and images on common phone screen sizes. Important actions should be easy to tap.

Trust and proof

Use genuine testimonials, real client-approved case studies, business contact details and accurate service information. Do not publish invented reviews or performance claims.

Technical SEO essentials

Use unique page titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, descriptive headings, crawlable links, a sitemap and useful image alt text. Keep pages fast and avoid duplicate or thin location pages.

Measure enquiries

Track meaningful actions such as calls, WhatsApp clicks and form completions. Review the data to understand which pages help visitors take action.
