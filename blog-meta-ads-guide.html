Facebook and Instagram Ads: a guide for businesses

Learn the basics of Meta advertising: campaign objectives, audiences, creative testing, budgets and enquiry measurement.

A practical guide from go2ads. Information is educational; the right approach depends on your business, market and goals.

Choose an objective that matches your goal

Decide whether you need awareness, traffic, leads, messages or sales. The campaign objective influences delivery optimization, so choose one that reflects the action you want people to take.

Know who you want to reach

Describe your ideal customer by location, needs and interests where relevant. Use audience settings thoughtfully and avoid narrowing the audience without a clear reason.

Create useful, specific ad creative

Show the product or service clearly. Explain the customer problem, what makes the offer relevant and what action to take next. Test different images, videos and messages instead of assuming one creative will work for everyone.

Make the next step simple

If you want messages, make sure WhatsApp or Messenger is monitored. If you want website leads, send people to a page that matches the ad and has a clear contact route.

Review performance regularly

Monitor spend, reach, clicks, cost per result and—most importantly—lead quality. Results depend on the offer, audience, creative, competition and follow-up process.
