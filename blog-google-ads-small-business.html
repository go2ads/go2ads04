Google Ads for small businesses: a practical starter guide

A practical introduction to Google Ads for small businesses: search intent, keywords, campaign structure, budgets and measurement.

A practical guide from go2ads. Information is educational; the right approach depends on your business, market and goals.

Start with the customer’s search

Think about the phrases a potential customer uses when they are ready to compare or buy. Separate high-intent searches from broad research terms, and make sure the landing page answers the search clearly.

Choose a focused campaign objective

Decide what a valuable action means for your business: a qualified phone call, enquiry form, WhatsApp conversation or purchase. Avoid optimizing only for clicks if the real goal is enquiries.

Build a relevant keyword list

Group closely related searches together. Review search terms after launch and exclude irrelevant queries where appropriate. Keyword match types and campaign settings affect which searches can trigger ads.

Set a budget you can evaluate

Choose a test budget that your business can sustain long enough to gather useful data. Actual costs vary by industry, competition, location, bidding strategy and ad quality; no fixed cost or result can be guaranteed.

Measure outcomes, not impressions alone

Track enquiry actions and assess lead quality. Compare campaign spend with meaningful business outcomes, and make changes based on enough data rather than reacting to every daily fluctuation.
